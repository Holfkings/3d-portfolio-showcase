About

The artist behind the renders.

I'm Alex — a 3D artist and animator based in Bogotá, Colombia. I specialize in character design, environment art, and product visualization, with a strong focus on photorealistic rendering and expressive motion.

My background is in industrial design, which gave me a foundation in form, proportion, and materials. Over the past 6 years I've worked with animation studios, ad agencies, and independent clients to create everything from stylized characters to photoreal product renders.

I believe great 3D art is about storytelling — every shape, light, and texture should serve the narrative.
